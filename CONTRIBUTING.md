# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome for reproducibility fixes, documentation, and research extensions.

Please keep three layers separate in contributions:

1. manuscript-reproduction code;
2. independent human-validation material;
3. future anatomical/clinical extensions.

Do not introduce clinical claims that are unsupported by explicit labels or independent validation. New numerical results should state their split, seeds, preprocessing fit scope, and whether they are in-sample, held-out, or post-hoc.
