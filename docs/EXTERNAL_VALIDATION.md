# External human validation

The manuscript separates two human-facing activities:

1. **Author-side focused feedback** on 40 representative final-rule exemplars. This is the feedback used in the reported semantic-calibration/generalization experiments.
2. **Independent blinded dental audit**, prepared as a separate evaluation of cluster coherence, Grad-CAM plausibility, and rule-history understandability/usefulness. The manuscript does not treat this independent audit as completed evidence.

The folder `validation/paper_audit_protocol/` contains reviewer-facing blank forms and instructions for the blinded paper audit.

The two ZIPs in `validation/anatomical_hitl/packages/` are a later extension that asks richer anatomical questions. They are included for continuity of the research program but are **not inputs to the results reported in the current manuscript**.
